Colour Change · Picnic App

Add before/after screens of the product backgrounds

Problem

White product backgrounds resulted in a flat, undifferentiated product presentation among hundreds of similar items in the Picnic app. Products were harder to tell apart at a glance, and the visual experience felt generic compared to the rest of the app.

Approach

I led the redesign to coloured product backgrounds, working closely with UX designers, developers, and stakeholders to explore whether colour could improve product distinction and presentation without harming clarity or brand consistency.

Add wireframes, colour explorations, or before/after comparisons here.

Validating it

Before a full rollout, I set up A/B tests to determine the most effective approach and validate that the new backgrounds actually improved the product presentation, rather than relying on assumptions alone.

Result

The redesign was rolled out across the full product assortment in the Netherlands, Germany, and France, validated through A/B testing ahead of new category launches.
